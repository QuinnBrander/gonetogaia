When you arrive at the meeting point, you find Father 
Flores giving a sermon to a group of children. They’re far too 
young to take the delving aptitude test, so you wonder why 
they’re so close to the shore. Curious, you listen in. 
“My children, do you see that beautiful light in the distance? 
That is Gaia.” A boy raises his hand. “Father, what is Gaia?”
“What a wonderful question! Gaia is a manifestation of the 
Earth. She came to us in a time of need to guide us towards 
salvation. Someday, one of you may have the honor of 
becoming a delver. If it is your fate, Gaia will guide you to 
her.” The children are spellbound, absorbing every word.
You hear footsteps behind you and turn to see Master Bram. 
At his side floats a peculiar robot. Father Flores quickly 
wraps up his sermon and his two attendants corral the 
attendees away.
“They get younger and younger every year,” Bram murmurs. 
Father Flores watches as the children return to town. “With 
the way things have gone lately, it’s best to prepare them 
early”. Bram nods.
Flores hands you a pair of binoculars and directs your 
eyeline to the outer perimeter. “Delvers, do you see 
anything unusual?” After a few minutes, you find it - a 
gap in the net of surveillance cameras floating just above 
the canopy.
“One of my disciples at the watchtower recently detected a 
disturbance at this location, and as you can see, we’ve lost 
a surveillance device. Recovering the camera would allow 
us to review its hard drive and potentially discover what 
brought it down. With luck, we may even be able to repair it. 
I appreciate your help with this vital mission.”
“Delvers,” Master Bram says, his voice steady. “The path 
ahead takes you deeper into the Tide, where great perils 
lurk. From now on, you won’t go alone.” He draws your 
attention to the squat, humming machine at his side. “This 
is Witness. He’s useless in a fight, but he’ll haul your gear 
for you. And should you fall in the mist, he’ll bring your 
bodies home.”
The name stirs a memory - you learned about Witness 
during your studies at the Academy. It’s said that no delver 
has ever reached Gaia without his help. Witness greets you 
by way of a soft whirr. He’s oddly expressive.
“Chin up, delvers,” Master Bram adds, with a grin. “And 
remember, if things get dicey, there’s no shame in turning 
back.” You don your delversuits and descend into the mist. 
Witness follows behind in silence - your new companion on 
the long road ahead.

The Burned Watchtower
As you approach the crowd that has grown around the 
watchtower, intense flames burst out and the old structure 
begins to crumble into itself. It is clear the fire had started 
burning inside the tower long before the alarms were set 
off. The crowd scatters and watches solemnly as the tower 
comes down, pieces flying off the cliff and disappearing 
into the mist. You quickly join the townsfolk in putting out 
the fire. Hours later, you spot Master Bram surveying the 
damage and join him.
“Thank Gaia that Dr Ira was awake to sound the alarm. 
This could have been so much worse. Only two people are 
unaccounted for - the Brass siblings. Vera was on guard 
duty, and we assume her brother Elias was up late doing 
research. I have to believe they’re still alive. Here is a photo 
of them. If you manage to find anything - any clues as to 
what may have caused this, I’d appreciate the help.” He 
hands you the photo.
 Add Key Item #6 to your Key Items.
You begin to survey the area. Nearby, you watch as Dr Ira 
and her attendants set up triage for the injured. Not far 
off, you notice Father Flores surrounded by his followers, 
heads bowed in prayer. Concerned townsfolk approach 
him to receive his blessings. He attends each one before 
approaching you with a pained smile.
“What a grim way to start your journey, delvers. Yet you 
must remain strong in moments like this. The people of 
Bird’s Nest depend on the delvers in more ways than you 
can imagine.”
You tell Father Flores that you’ve been tasked with finding 
any clues on what caused the fire. At this, he frowns slightly, 
but quickly replaces it with a kind smile. “But of course, my 
child. I fear the fire may have consumed everything, but I 
have full faith that if there’s anything to be found, you will 
do so.”
You begin your search of the watchtower ruins, still warm 
from the heat of the fire.
